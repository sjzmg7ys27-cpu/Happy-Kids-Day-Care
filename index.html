Safety & Care Guidelines

For the safety, care & happiness of every child

✅ DO'S

  • Bring your child clean and neatly dressed.
  • Pack a labelled bag containing water bottle, healthy snacks/lunch, extra pair of clothes, diapers/wipes (if required), personal towel.
  • Inform staff about allergies, medical conditions, medication (written instructions required), emergency contact number.
  • Drop off and pick up your child on time.
  • Label all personal belongings.
  • Notify the centre if your child will be absent.

❌ DON'TS

  • Do not send your child if they have fever, vomiting, diarrhoea, contagious illness, severe cough or flu symptoms.
  • Do not send expensive jewellery, toys or valuables.
  • Do not send sharp objects, medicines (without written authorization) or unsafe food items.
  • Do not allow unauthorized persons to collect your child.
  • Do not bring your child after the scheduled admission time without informing the centre.

🛡️ IMPORTANT SAFETY RULES & CARD INSTRUCTIONS

  • Children will only be released to authorized persons. Parents must sign the attendance register during drop-off and pick-up.
  • The admission card is mandatory during drop-off and pick-up. Keep the admission card safe (a replacement card may require a fee).
  • Any medication will only be administered with written parental consent and clear dosage instructions.
  • Parents must inform the centre immediately about any change in contact details or emergency contacts.
  • The centre reserves the right to verify the identity of anyone collecting the child.
